About

I hold an MS in Computer Science from Air University, Islamabad, and I am currently a PhD candidate in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics at the Slovak University of Technology in Bratislava. My research covers gait event analysis, human posture and motion analysis, surveillance, 3D reconstruction, e-learning, behavior prediction, and information security.

I am passionate about combining robotics with advanced computational methods to build practical solutions, including smart city systems that bring together artificial intelligence, image processing and hardware. I have taught at university level for several years and have published in IEEE and MDPI venues.
